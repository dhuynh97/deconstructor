## Critical Finding: This Is NOT a Condo

**4350 Kirkham Street is a multi-unit rental apartment building, not a condominium.** Individual units (including Apt 303) are **not separately subdivided** and therefore **cannot be purchased individually** on the open market. The entire 24-unit building is owned as a single parcel.

To purchase Apt 303 specifically, one of the following would need to occur:
1. **Condo conversion** - The owner converts the building to condominiums (subject to SF condo conversion lottery and regulations)
2. **TIC (Tenancy in Common)** purchase - A fractional ownership arrangement (less common, harder to finance)
3. **Purchase the entire building** - Buy all 24 units as a single property
